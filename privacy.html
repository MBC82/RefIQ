Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026  ·  RefIQ LLC  ·  Kansas City, Missouri

This Privacy Policy explains how RefIQ LLC ("RefIQ," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at www.myrefiq.com, the RefIQ iPhone app, and related services. By using RefIQ, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication).

Profile & Usage Data. We collect information about your subscription plan, quiz results, scores, and training activity to provide the service and generate analytics for league administrators.

League & Referee Data. League administrators may upload rule documents and create quiz links. Referee names entered when taking a quiz are collected and associated with quiz results.

Payment Information. Subscriptions to the RefIQ iPhone app are billed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your card number or billing address. Apple tells us whether a subscription is active so we can unlock the app's features.

Technical Data. We automatically collect your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited when you use our service. This data is used for security, performance monitoring, and debugging.

The RefIQ App: What We Collect

The RefIQ app is a scheduling tool. Assignors post games and assign officials; officials see, accept, or decline their assignments. If you use the app, we collect:

  • Account. Name, email address, and a password handled by Google Firebase Authentication (we never see it). If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us your name and either your real email or a private relay address, as you choose.
  • Official profile (mostly optional). Phone number, photo, home zip code, sports, age groups, skill types, positions, certification level, badge number and expiry, and years of experience, so assignors can match you to the right games.
  • Availability. Weekly availability windows, recurring unavailable times, and days off.
  • Assignments. The games you are assigned to and whether you accepted or declined.
  • Post-game reports. Officials may file reports with the final score, cards issued, injuries, sideline behavior ratings, field conditions, and incident descriptions.
  • Assignors. Your organization's name, logo and color, the games and roster you create, and any private ratings or notes you write about officials.

The app does not collect your precise location, contacts, health records, or advertising identifiers, and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

The RefIQ App: Who Can See Your Information

  • Your assignor (for each league you join) can see your profile, availability, photo, phone number, and email, plus your assignments, responses, and reports.
  • Other officials on the same game see only your name and position.
  • Post-game reports are visible only to the assignor and the official who wrote them. They are never shown to clubs, coaches, or other officials. League summaries an assignor chooses to share with a board show clubs and teams only, with no official names, and hide averages based on fewer than three ratings.
  • Ratings and notes an assignor writes about an official are visible only to that assignor. The official sees only an average and how many ratings it is based on.

If you tap Add to calendar, the app asks permission to add events only; it cannot read your other calendar events. If you pick a profile photo, the app receives only that photo, shrinks it to a small square, and stores that copy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and subscription
  • To let assignors schedule games and officials respond to assignments in the app
  • To deliver AI-generated quiz questions and training content
  • To show league administrators quiz results and referee performance data
  • To process payments and send transactional emails (receipts, plan updates)
  • To improve our AI models, question quality, and platform features
  • To send service-related communications (we do not send marketing emails without consent)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate RefIQ. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and database (Firestore). Data is stored on Google's infrastructure. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic, PBC — AI quiz generation via the Claude API. Quiz prompts (including uploaded league rule documents) are sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Vercel, Inc. — Website hosting and serverless functions. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc. — Sign in with Apple, for app users who choose it. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — Font delivery. Your browser connects to Google's servers to load fonts.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your league administrator. If you take a quiz through a league-issued link, your name and quiz results are visible to the league administrator who created that quiz.
  • Service providers. We share data with Firebase, Apple, Anthropic, and Vercel solely to operate the service, as described above.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of RefIQ and its users.
  • Business transfers. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and quiz data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain records for legal or financial compliance purposes (e.g., records of App Store subscription transactions).

Quiz results associated with a league may be retained by that league administrator independent of your account status.

Deleting your account in the app. Open the app, go to Account → Delete my account, and follow the steps. We delete your profile, sign-in, roster entries, development records, and assignments. Two things remain on purpose: game reports you wrote stay with the league with your name removed, and private ratings or notes an assignor wrote about you stay in that assignor's records. If you are an assignor and delete your account, your league and everything in it is deleted. If you signed in with Apple, we also disconnect RefIQ from your Apple ID.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access. You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction. You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion. You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Opt-out of marketing. You may opt out of any marketing communications at any time.
  • California residents (CCPA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of any sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at refiqsupport@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & Tracking

RefIQ uses browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) to maintain your login session via Firebase Authentication. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. Google Fonts may set cookies when delivering fonts to your browser.

8. Children's Privacy

RefIQ is designed for use by adult league administrators and referees. The RefIQ app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Note: Many youth officials are teenagers. If you are under the age of majority where you live, make sure a parent or guardian is comfortable with you using the RefIQ app. Post-game reports should describe team and sideline behavior and should not name or describe individual children. Referees who take league quizzes may also be minors. In this case, the league administrator is responsible for ensuring appropriate consent has been obtained under applicable law.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including Firebase Authentication, HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, and Firestore Security Rules to restrict data access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
