What is inside

EVERYTHING AN ASSIGNOR ACTUALLY DOES

Auto-assign

FILL CREWS IN MINUTES

Matches officials to open slots using availability, age group, skill type, position, travel time, and how many games each person has had. You review every suggestion before anything is sent.

Conflicts

CATCH IT BEFORE GAME DAY

Flags double-bookings, overlapping venues, and duplicate games, plus officials who are not free at that time or have the day off.

Import

BRING YOUR SCHEDULE

Drop in a CSV, a PDF, a screenshot, or text from an email. RefIQ reads it and builds the games that need officials.

Availability

EXACT TIMES, NOT GUESSES

Officials set the hours they can work each day, block recurring times, and mark days off. Assignors see a fit badge on every pick.

Reports

Officials file scores, cards, injuries, and sideline behavior. Reports stay private to the assignor, and board summaries show clubs only.

Development

Certification expiry warnings, at-risk officials, retention insights, and feedback tracked per official.

Auto-assign, with you in charge

A PROPOSAL, NOT A SURPRISE

Auto-assign never sends anything on its own. It builds a proposal, shows you why each official was picked, and tells you plainly when no one can take a slot.

  • Explains every pick. Free at that time, prefers that age group, games so far this season.
  • Explains every gap. "No one can take it: 4 already on another game, 3 not free at that time."
  • Swap anyone. Change a suggestion or leave a slot open, then send them all at once.

Questions

COMMON QUESTIONS

Which sports does it support?

Soccer, basketball, baseball, softball, football, volleyball, hockey, lacrosse, and rugby, each with preset crew sizes. Anything else works too: add your own sport and positions.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. RefIQ is on iPhone first. Email us if Android matters to your league.

How do officials join my league?

You share a six-character league code. Officials enter it in the app under Account and appear on your roster.

Who can see post-game reports and ratings?

Reports are visible only to the assignor and the official who wrote them. Ratings and notes you write about an official are private to you; the official sees only an average. Summaries you share with a board show clubs only, with no official names.

Can I import my existing schedule?

Yes. Import a CSV, a PDF, a photo of a schedule, or pasted text. RefIQ builds the games and flags duplicates so you can review before anything is created.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, from inside the app under Account → Delete my account. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what is removed.
