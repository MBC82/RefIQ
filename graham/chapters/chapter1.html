Simplifying and mixed numbers

Builds on Review questions 1–2

1.
ExplainWithout doing any division, how can you tell 721 is not simplified but 73 is?
Show answer

7 and 21 share a factor of 7. 7 and 3 share nothing but 1.

A fraction is simplified when the numerator and denominator have no common factor other than 1. It has nothing to do with whether the fraction is bigger than 1 — 73 is improper but still simplified.

2.
Find the mistakeA student simplified 1218 and got 46, then stopped. What's wrong, and what's the correct answer?
Show answer

Not finished. 1218 = 23

4 and 6 still share a factor of 2. Dividing by the greatest common factor (6) gets you there in one step.

Watch out: Always check your answer one more time: can both numbers still be divided by the same thing?

3.
True or false“If the denominator is an odd number, the fraction is already simplified.”
Show answer

False. Example: 915 = 35

Odd numbers can share odd factors like 3, 5, or 7. Only a shared factor matters.

4.
ExplainExplain why 614 equals 254 — not the shortcut, the reason.
Show answer

6 wholes = 24 fourths, plus 1 more fourth = 25 fourths

Each whole is 44, so 6 wholes is 244. The shortcut (6 × 4 + 1) is just counting those fourths quickly.

5.
Go backwardsWrite 385 as a mixed number.
Show answer

735

5 goes into 38 seven times (35), with 3 fifths left over.

6.
Odd one outWhich one is not equal to the others?
312,  72,  144,  217
Show answer

217 — it equals 3, the rest equal 3½

144 simplifies to 72, which is 312. But 217 = 3 exactly.

Absolute value

Builds on Review questions 3, 4, 6

7.
ExplainWhat does absolute value actually measure? Use that to explain why it can never be negative.
Show answer

Distance from 0 on the number line

Distance is always zero or positive — you can't walk −5 steps. So |−8| and |8| are both 8 because both numbers are 8 steps from 0.

8.
Watch the signEvaluate:  −|−30|
Show answer

−30

Do the bars first: |−30| = 30. The negative sign outside the bars is still waiting, so the answer is −30.

Watch out: The bars only make what's inside them non-negative. A sign outside the bars stays.

9.
Think it throughName every number whose absolute value is 9. Then: is there any number with absolute value −9?
Show answer

9 and −9.  No number has |x| = −9.

Two numbers are 9 steps from zero — one on each side. No distance can be negative.

10.
True or false“If one number is less than another, its absolute value is also less.”
Show answer

False. −7 < 2, but |−7| > |2|

Negative numbers get smaller as they get farther from 0, but their absolute value gets bigger.

11.
CompareFill in <, >, or =.
a.  −8 ___ −3     b.  |−8| ___ |−3|
Why do the two answers point in opposite directions?
Show answer

a.  −8 < −3    b.  8 > 3

−8 is farther left on the number line, so it's less. But it's farther from zero, so its absolute value is greater.

12.
OrderOrder from least to greatest:
|−6|,  −6,  0,  |4|,  −|−2|
Show answer

−6,  −|−2|,  0,  |4|,  |−6|

Rewrite each value first: 6, −6, 0, 4, −2. Then order: −6, −2, 0, 4, 6.

Watch out: Don't skip rewriting. −|−2| is −2, not 2.

Integers in real situations

Builds on Review questions 7, 18

13.
Write the integerWrite an integer for each:
a. 40 feet below sea level
b. The temperature rose 12 degrees
c. You withdraw $25 from the bank
Show answer

a. −40   b. +12   c. −25

Look for direction words. Below, down, lose, withdraw, back → negative. Above, up, gain, deposit, forward → positive.

14.
ExplainWhich is colder: −15°F or −4°F? Explain using a number line, not just a rule.
Show answer

−15°F is colder

On a number line, −15 sits to the left of −4. Farther left means less, and less means colder.

15.
Word problemA diver is 12 feet below the surface. She rises 7 feet, then dives down 10 feet. Write an expression, then find her position.
Show answer

−12 + 7 − 10 = −15  (15 ft below)

Start at −12. Rising 7 gets to −5. Diving 10 more gets to −15.

Adding integers

Builds on Review questions 5, 8, 9, 12

16.
PredictWithout adding, will −45 + 30 be positive, negative, or zero? Explain how you know.
Show answer

Negative

The signs are different, so the answer takes the sign of the number farther from zero. |−45| is bigger than |30|, so the negative “wins.”

17.
ExplainThe review showed 30 + (−30) = 0. When does adding a positive and a negative give exactly zero?
Show answer

When they're opposites (same distance from 0)

Numbers like 30 and −30 are called additive inverses. They cancel out completely.

18.
Find the mistakeA student says: “Two negatives make a positive, so −8 + (−5) = 13.” What's wrong?
Show answer

−8 + (−5) = −13

Adding two negatives moves you farther left on the number line, so the sum is negative. Add the distances (8 + 5) and keep the negative sign.

Watch out: “Two negatives make a positive” is about subtracting a negative (and later, multiplying). It is not a rule for adding.

19.
Missing numberFind the missing number.
a. −17 + ___ = 0
b. 23 + ___ = 5
Show answer

a. 17    b. −18

a: the opposite of −17 cancels it. b: to get from 23 down to 5 you have to move 18 to the left, which is adding −18.

20.
Always, sometimes, never“The sum of a positive number and a negative number is negative.”
Show answer

Sometimes

−10 + 3 = −7 (negative), but −3 + 10 = 7 (positive), and −5 + 5 = 0. It depends on which number is farther from zero.

Subtracting integers

Builds on Review questions 10, 11, 13

21.
RewriteRewrite each subtraction as addition. Don't solve yet.
a. −25 − (−5)    b. 23 − 98    c. 65 − (−45)
Show answer

a. −25 + 5   b. 23 + (−98)   c. 65 + 45

Subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite. Keep the first number, change subtraction to addition, flip the sign of the second number.

22.
ExplainWhy does subtracting a negative number make the result bigger? Use money or a real example.
Show answer

Taking away a debt leaves you better off

If someone erases a $5 debt you owed (subtracting −5), your net worth goes up by $5. Removing something negative is a positive change.

23.
Find the mistakeA student wrote 65 − (−45) = 20. What did they do wrong?
Show answer

Correct answer: 110

They treated it like 65 − 45. Subtracting −45 means adding 45: 65 + 45 = 110.

Watch out: When you see two negative-looking signs side by side, slow down and rewrite as addition first.

24.
CompareFind 7 − 12 and 12 − 7. What do you notice? Does order matter in subtraction?
Show answer

−5 and 5.  Yes, order matters.

Switching the order gives you the opposite answer. Addition doesn't care about order; subtraction does.

25.
DistanceHow far apart are −9 and 14 on a number line? Write it as a subtraction problem.
Show answer

14 − (−9) = 23

From −9 to 0 is 9 steps, from 0 to 14 is 14 more. 9 + 14 = 23. Subtracting (bigger − smaller) finds that distance in one step.

Fractions and mixed numbers with signs

Builds on Review questions 14–15

26.
Estimate firstBefore solving, will −23 + (−34) be greater than or less than −1? Then solve.
Show answer

Less than −1.  Answer: −1512

Both numbers are more than half a unit left of zero, so together they pass −1. Common denominator 12: −812 + (−912) = −1712 = −1512.

27.
Estimate first−314 − 4910: about what should the answer be before you calculate? Then find the exact answer.
Show answer

About −8.  Exact: −8320

−3 and a bit, minus 4 and almost 1 more, lands just past −8. Exact: −134 − 4910 = −6520 − 9820 = −16320 = −8320.

Watch out: If your exact answer isn't close to your estimate, something went wrong — check it before moving on.

28.
Find the mistakeA student wrote −12 + 34 = 26. Find two things wrong.
Show answer

Correct answer: 14

1) You can't add denominators — you need a common one: −24 + 34. 2) The signs were ignored. The positive 34 is farther from zero, so the answer is positive 14.

29.
Solve56 − (−13)
Show answer

76 = 116

Subtracting a negative means adding: 56 + 26 = 76.

30.
Predict, then solve−212 + 413: is the answer positive or negative? Then solve.
Show answer

Positive.  156

413 is farther from zero, so the answer is positive. −156 + 266 = 116 = 156.

Multi-step problems and range

Builds on Review questions 16–19

31.
Check your thinkingSolve 7 + (−11) + 8 two different ways: left to right, then by adding the positives first. Do you get the same answer? Why?
Show answer

4 both ways

Left to right: 7 + (−11) = −4, then −4 + 8 = 4. Positives first: 7 + 8 = 15, then 15 + (−11) = 4. When everything is addition, you can reorder.

32.
ExplainWithout a calculator, explain why −8 − (−6) + 2 equals 0.
Show answer

−8 + 6 + 2 = −8 + 8 = 0

Rewrite the subtraction as + 6. The 6 and 2 together make +8, which cancels −8 exactly.

33.
RangeLow temperatures for a week:
MonTueWedThuFri
−74−129−3
Find the lowest, highest, and range. Then explain why the range is not 9 − 12.
Show answer

Low −12, high 9, range 9 − (−12) = 21

Range is the distance between highest and lowest, so it can't be negative. 9 − 12 = −3 forgets that the lowest value is −12, not 12.

Watch out: Write the negative number in parentheses: 9 − (−12). It keeps you from dropping the sign.

34.
Word problemYour account is at −$40. You deposit $65, then a $30 charge comes out. Write one expression and find the balance.
Show answer

−40 + 65 − 30 = −5

−40 + 65 = 25, then 25 − 30 = −5. Still overdrawn by $5.

35.
Work backwardsA set of numbers has a range of 50. The lowest value is −18. What is the highest value?
Show answer

32

Highest − (−18) = 50, so highest + 18 = 50, so highest = 32. Check: 32 − (−18) = 50.

36.
Create your ownWrite a subtraction problem using two negative numbers where the answer is positive. Explain why it works.
Show answer

Example: −3 − (−10) = 7

It becomes −3 + 10. It works whenever the number being subtracted is farther left (more negative) than the first number. If you can build examples like this, you really understand subtracting negatives.
