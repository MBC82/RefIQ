Mixed numbers and improper fractions

Builds on Review questions 1–3

1.
ExplainWhat's the difference between a mixed number and an improper fraction? Which is 73, and why isn't it called “wrong”?
Show answer

Mixed number = whole + fraction. 73 is an improper fraction.

Improper just means the numerator is at least as big as the denominator, so the value is 1 or more. Nothing is wrong with it — it's often easier to compute with.

2.
Find the mistakeA student wrote: −729 = −639 + 29 = −619. What's wrong, and what's the right answer?
Show answer

−659

The negative applies to the whole amount, not just the 7. −729 means −(7 + 29) = −659. Convert the positive part first, then put the negative sign back on.

Watch out: Any time a mixed number is negative, convert 729 first and attach the minus sign at the end.

3.
ExplainConvert −3616 to a mixed number in simplest form. Could you simplify before converting? Try both orders.
Show answer

−214

Convert first: −2416, then simplify 416 to 14. Or simplify first: 3616 = 94, then convert to 214. Same answer either way.

4.
True or false“Every improper fraction is greater than 1.”
Show answer

False

33 is improper but equals exactly 1, and −73 is improper but is less than 1 because it's negative. Improper describes the form (top ≥ bottom), not the size.

5.
CompareWhich is greater: −214 or −212? Explain with a number line.
Show answer

−214 is greater

Both are between −2 and −3. −212 is farther left, so it's less. With negatives, the bigger fractional part means the smaller number.

Watch out: Don't just compare 14 and 12 and pick the bigger one — the negative flips it.

Sign rules for multiplying and dividing

Builds on Review questions 5–8

6.
ExplainWhy does a negative times a negative give a positive? Finish the pattern and see:
−3 × 2 = −6,   −3 × 1 = −3,   −3 × 0 = 0,   −3 × (−1) = ___,   −3 × (−2) = ___
Show answer

3 and 6

Each step to the left adds 3 to the answer. To keep the pattern going past zero, the products have to turn positive.

7.
PredictWithout multiplying, is −12(5)(−3) positive or negative? What rule are you using?
Show answer

Positive — two negative factors

Count the negative factors. An even number of negatives gives a positive product; an odd number gives a negative.

8.
PredictDecide the sign of each without computing:
a. (−2)(−3)(−4)    b. (−2)(−3)(−4)(−5)
Show answer

a. Negative    b. Positive

Three negatives is odd → negative. Four negatives is even → positive. The numbers themselves don't matter for the sign.

9.
CompareFind each and explain why two of them match:
a. {−80/20}    b. {80/−20}    c. {−80/−20}
Show answer

a. −4   b. −4   c. 4

One negative (wherever it sits) makes the quotient negative. Two negatives cancel out.

10.
True or false“When you divide one integer by another, the answer is always an integer.”
Show answer

False

7 ÷ 2 = 3.5. Division doesn't always come out even, which is exactly why fractions exist. (Multiplying two integers does always give an integer.)

Multiplying and dividing integers

Builds on Review questions 5–8

11.
Missing numberFill in the blanks.
a. −7 × ___ = 56    b. ___ ÷ (−5) = −9
Show answer

a. −8    b. 45

a: the product is positive, so the missing factor must be negative. b: the quotient is negative, so the dividend and divisor have opposite signs.

12.
Find the mistakeA student wrote −3 • (−40) = −120, saying “a negative sign carries through.” What's wrong?
Show answer

−3 • (−40) = 120

Two negative factors cancel. The sign rule for multiplying isn't the same as for adding, where −3 + (−40) really is −43.

13.
CompareAre 100 ÷ (−5) and (−100) ÷ 5 the same? What about (−100) ÷ (−5)?
Show answer

First two both −20. The third is 20.

It doesn't matter which number carries the negative — one negative makes the quotient negative. Two negatives make it positive.

14.
Word problemThe temperature drops 4 degrees every hour for 6 hours. Write a multiplication expression with an integer, and find the total change.
Show answer

−4 × 6 = −24 degrees

A repeated drop is repeated addition of a negative, which is multiplication by a negative. The answer is a change of −24, not a temperature of −24.

Order of operations

Builds on Review questions 9–10, 13

15.
ExplainIn 40 ÷ (−5) + (−19), which operation comes first and why? What answer do you get?
Show answer

Divide first. −8 + (−19) = −27

Multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction, no matter which is written first.

16.
Find the mistakeA student evaluated −10 − 16 × (−2) by subtracting first: −26 × (−2) = 52. What's wrong, and what's the correct answer?
Show answer

22

Multiply first: 16 × (−2) = −32. Then −10 − (−32) = −10 + 32 = 22.

Watch out: Going left to right is only safe within the same level of operations.

17.
Work backwardsWhere would you put parentheses in −10 − 16 × (−2) to make the answer 52?
Show answer

(−10 − 16) × (−2)

Parentheses outrank everything, so they force the subtraction to happen first: −26 × (−2) = 52. That's why the student's wrong answer was 52.

18.
CompareEvaluate both and explain why they differ:
a. −24 ÷ (−2 − 4)    b. −24 ÷ (−2) − 4
Show answer

a. 4    b. 8

In a, the parentheses make the divisor −6, so −24 ÷ (−6) = 4. In b, you divide first: 12, then subtract 4.

Multiplying and dividing fractions

Builds on Review questions 4, 11–13

19.
ExplainWhy does dividing by a fraction mean multiplying by its reciprocal? Use 12 ÷ 34 — what is that question really asking?
Show answer

How many 34s fit in 12?  16

Each whole holds 4 thirds-of-a-quarter... more simply: each whole holds 43 groups of 34, so 12 wholes hold 12 × 43 = 16. Dividing asks how many groups fit.

20.
Set it upWrite a multiplication expression for −89 ÷ 45. Don't solve it.
Show answer

−89 × 54

Keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, and flip the second one. Only the second fraction flips.

21.
ReciprocalsGive the reciprocal of each, and explain what makes two numbers reciprocals:
a. −56    b. 7    c. 112
Show answer

a. −65   b. 17   c. 23

Two numbers are reciprocals when their product is 1. The sign doesn't flip — only the numerator and denominator swap. Convert mixed numbers first: 112 = 32.

22.
PredictWithout computing: is 23 × 12 bigger or smaller than 23? Why?
Show answer

Smaller: 13

Multiplying by a number less than 1 takes a part of it, so the result shrinks. Multiplying by more than 1 grows it.

23.
PredictWithout computing: is 12 ÷ 34 bigger or smaller than 12? Why?
Show answer

Bigger: 16

Dividing asks how many groups fit, and the groups are smaller than 1, so more of them fit. Dividing by a number less than 1 always makes the result bigger.

24.
Two answersFind −213 • (−514). Give the answer as an improper fraction in simplest form and as a mixed number.
Show answer

494 = 1214

−73 • −214 = 14712. Two negatives make a positive. 14712 simplifies to 494, which is 1214.

Watch out: 14712 is correct but not simplified. If the directions say simplest form, keep reducing.

25.
Find the mistakeTo compute −345 ÷ 25, a student flipped the first fraction: 519 × 25. What's wrong, and what's the right answer?
Show answer

−195 × 52 = −192 = −912

Only the divisor (the second number) flips. The first number stays exactly as it is, negative sign and all.

26.
ExplainIn 3 + 916 ÷ (−56), which operation comes first, and what's the answer?
Show answer

Division first. 3 + (−11) = −8

556 ÷ (−56) = 556 × (−65) = −11. Then add 3.

Fractions as decimals

Builds on Review question 16

27.
ExplainHow do you turn 58 into a decimal? What does the fraction bar actually mean?
Show answer

5 ÷ 8 = 0.625

The fraction bar is a division sign. Top divided by bottom, always in that order.

28.
PredictOne of these is greater than 1: 58, 96, 945, 716. Which, and how can you tell without dividing?
Show answer

96

Its numerator is bigger than its denominator, so it's more than one whole. Its decimal is 1.5, the only one above 1.

29.
MatchMatch each fraction to its decimal:
58,  96,  945,  716
0.2,  0.625,  0.4375,  1.5
Show answer

58=0.625, 96=1.5, 945=0.2, 716=0.4375

Divide top by bottom for each. Shortcuts: 945 simplifies to 15 = 0.2, and 96 is more than 1, so it has to be 1.5.

30.
Reason it outWithout dividing, which is closer to 12: 716 or 916? How do you know?
Show answer

They're the same distance (116 each)

12 = 816. One is 116 below it, the other is 116 above it.

Word problems

Builds on Review questions 14–15

31.
Word problemHow many three-fourth ounce packages can be made from 12 ounces of peanuts? Explain why the answer is bigger than 12.
Show answer

12 ÷ 34 = 16 packages

Each package is smaller than an ounce, so you get more packages than ounces. Total ÷ size of each part = number of parts.

32.
Word problemYou burn 10 calories each minute you jog. What integer represents the change in your calories after jogging 20 minutes?
Show answer

−10 × 20 = −200 calories

Burning is a loss, so the rate is −10 per minute. The answer is a change of −200, not 200 calories gained.

33.
Word problemA diver descends 34 of a foot each second for 12 seconds. Write an expression with a negative number and find her depth change.
Show answer

−34 × 12 = −9 feet

Descending repeatedly is multiplying a negative rate by the time. She's 9 feet lower than where she started.

34.
Create your ownWrite your own word problem that requires dividing by a fraction, and show that the answer is bigger than the number you started with.
Show answer

Example: how many 13-cup scoops are in 5 cups? 5 ÷ 13 = 15

Any “how many small pieces fit in a bigger amount” question works. If you can write one of these yourself, you understand what dividing by a fraction means.
