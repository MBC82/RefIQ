Help

Support

We are a small team and reply within two business days.

Email refiqsupport@gmail.com with your account email and a short description of the problem. A screenshot helps.

How do officials join my league?

Give them the league code shown on the League tab. They enter it under Account → Join a league.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and we will email you a reset link. If you signed in with Apple, there is no password: tap Continue with Apple.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete my account. This removes your profile, roster entries, and assignments. See the Privacy Policy for what stays. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Who sees my post-game reports?

Only your assignor. Never clubs, coaches, or other officials.

I cannot see my games.

Check that you have joined your league (Account tab) and that your assignor has assigned you. Pull down on the schedule to refresh.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. RefIQ is on iPhone first.

I use RefIQ quiz training, not the app.

Email us at the address above and include your account email. Plans and billing are handled through Stripe.
